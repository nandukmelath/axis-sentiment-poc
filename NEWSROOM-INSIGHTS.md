# Newsroom UI — Insight Feature Backlog

Generated by a 6-lens brainstorm (fraud, comms/PR, product, CX-ops, data-science, exec),
deduped and adversarially data-vetted against `axis.db`. Every "VERIFIED" number was queried
live by the vetting agent. Feasibility: **backed** = data fully supports it · **partial** =
works on a subset · numbers are the honest ceiling.

Do NOT confuse tiers with the sketch — these are ON TOP of the already-planned post cards,
category rail, highlight rail, filters, search.

---

## MUST-HAVE (build these into v1)

### 1. Response-Gap War Room  ·  backed
Triage board of high-reach / high-stakes negative posts the bank **never answered**
(`n_bank_replies=0`), aged by hours-open, ranked by reach × urgency, green dot when a
reply-draft exists.
**Why:** `fact_interaction` = 588 threads, only **4** got any bank reply. The answer-gap IS the story.
**Caveat:** SLA clock is relative, not live (only 4 replies detected). Don't show resolution status — those columns are empty.

### 2. Red-Flag Desk (composite risk lane)  ·  backed
Fuses `churn_risk` + `fraud_signal` + intent (legal/churn/fraud) + `urgency` + `pii` into one
priority score; each high-consequence post → an action ticket with recommended team + action.
**Why:** the liability set = **76 posts** (fraud_report 42 + churn_threat 18 + legal_threat 16),
churn∩fraud overlap 43, legal+critical 3 — isolates them from the 1,046 praise posts.
**Caveat:** recommended_action/team populated on only 679/1,769 (38%); the 76 flagged are mostly LLM-scored so they carry it.

### 3. Urgency-Weighted Work Queue (triage sort)  ·  backed
A "Triage" sort toggle: rank every card by urgency × intent-weight × reach × severity,
open-first, driving factor as a chip.
**Why:** turns the chronological explorer into a worklist — the decision the analyst now makes by eye.
**Caveat:** 1,351/1,769 are urgency=low and 963 are vader-scored, so the queue TAIL is near-ties. Top of queue is trustworthy.

### 4. Fraud Fast-Lane + PII-redaction guard  ·  partial
Escalation lane on `fraud_signal=1`, bucketed by fraud_type, route-to-fraud_cyber CTA, red PII
guard masking the quote (`text_masked`) until confirmed.
**Why:** 126 fraud posts, 23 fraud_report critical; PII masking data exists (`text_masked` on 1,259 rows).
**Caveat:** fraud_type is 48 messy spellings (dominant = useless "suspected"); needs normalization.

### 5. Model Trust & Provenance  ·  backed  ·  ⭐ strongest data story
Per-card provenance bar from `confidence` + model-tier badge; **dims lexicon rows** with "shallow
read — verify before quoting"; sarcasm=1 rows render as polarity-flip cards.
**Why:** `model='vader-fast'` = 963 rows (54%), and **every one has emotion='joy'** — 100% degenerate.
Stops anyone quoting a flat lexicon guess as a press line. Sarcasm=1 = 11 posts, 10 truly negative.
**Fix:** lexicon confidence floor is **0.40**, not 0.42 — correct any copy.

## HIGH-VALUE

### 6. Thread Reconstructor + "did we ever reply?"  ·  backed
Rebuild any `conversation_id` thread as a vertical timeline colored by score → sentiment
trajectory; overlay `fact_interaction` to mark bank replies. **126 threads; largest = 33 posts / 32 authors.**
Caveat: 884 posts have no conversation_id (non-Twitter/Reddit); reply overlay lights on only 4 threads.

### 7. Influencer / Verified-Voice Watchtower  ·  backed
Reach-ranked watchlist from `mart_influencers`, yellow CHECK flag when a high-reach positive
score contradicts a scathing message. **@Tharun1: reach 143k, +0.747 avg but flagged scathing.**
Caveat: only 25 influencers; the value is the mismatch flag, not volume.

### 8. Regulator & Legal-Exposure Radar  ·  backed
Board of posts tagging regulators / threatening legal, showing which authority + escalation verb.
**~266 posts reference RBI/ombudsman/FinMin/Sitharaman; 16 explicit legal_threat.** A generic tool can't parse Indian regulator handles.
Caveat: regex needs a curated handle list (false positives on any "RBI" substring).

### 9. Reply-Draft Assist drawer  ·  partial
Side drawer with the pre-generated `reply_drafts.draft` + recommended_action + aspect evidence,
editable + copy. **65 drafts exist, all on high/critical posts.**
Caveat: only 65 of ~588 open issues have a draft; rest need on-demand generation.

### 10. Earned-Media / Press-Coverage Lane  ·  backed  ·  NEW
Separate journalist/news items from customer social — outlet, headline sentiment, "brief
leadership" action (you don't reply to an article). **~238 press items are currently dumped into
the same feed as customer tweets.** Different handling protocol, invisible when mixed.

### 11. Team Load-Balancer / Routing board  ·  partial
Regroup the filtered feed by `recommended_team` (open / #critical / #fraud / avg sentiment),
mirroring `mart_team_queue`. **fraud_cyber = 63 open / 40 critical / 61 fraud / avg −0.795.**
Caveat: recommended_team='none' on **61.6%** of rows — lean on the aggregate panel, not per-card chips.

### 12. Trend Anomaly Rail  ·  partial  ·  NEW — fixes a real gap
"What spiked today vs baseline" from `mart_trends.z_score/anomaly` + a `mart_forecast` peek.
**This is the data-backed emerging signal** — the planned emerging-cluster banner returns ZERO rows
(no cluster meets the rule). Use this instead.
Caveat: only 19 trend rows over 4 months — short history.

### 13. Peer Negativity Leaderboard  ·  partial
Axis vs HDFC/ICICI/SBI/Kotak by pct_negative + a "sentiment-quality gap" delta.
**Axis 25.7% negative vs HDFC 10.5% / Kotak 4.7% (SBI also high 19.8%).**
Caveat: collection asymmetric (Axis 1,769 vs rivals 64–81); ONLY pct_negative/avg_score comparable — grey out SOV.

### 14. Serial-Complainant Handle Dossier  ·  backed
Click a handle → their whole footprint (#fraud/#complaint/#legal/#churn, sample strip, "Day N"
campaign timeline). Distinguishes a one-off tweet from a sustained campaigner.
Caveat: sort by post-count/intent, not churn_prob (it's saturated at ~1.0).

### 15. Revenue-at-Risk & Churn Save-List  ·  partial
Pin churn_threat + legal_threat posts (34), matched to `mart_churn_risk`, LTV multiplier → an
"estimated exposure" figure.
Caveat: churn rows are Twitter HANDLES not CRM customers (dim_customer is 6 synthetic); rupee figure is directional.

### 16. Coordination / Brigading Detector  ·  partial
Read `conversation_id` author-diversity to separate a genuine pile-on (many authors) from a
single-actor flood. **33-post thread = 32 distinct authors = genuine outrage.**
Caveat: the "copy-paste storm" half is empty (only 2 duplicate groups have >1 author) — ship the brigading view only.

### 17. Fraud Taxonomy Rollup  ·  partial
Collapse the 48 raw fraud_type spellings into ~10 clean families, each a drill-through tile.
Caveat: even normalized, most families are tiny; dominant label is useless "suspected" (47). Directional taxonomy, not volumes.

### 18. Aspect-evidence "show your work" highlighter  ·  partial
For posts with `aspects_json`, render each {aspect, sentiment, **evidence**} as a chip that
highlights the exact span in the post body — auditable sentiment. **705 posts carry evidence quotes.**
Caveat: per-card ONLY. An aggregate aspect×sentiment matrix is worthless (721 of 801 rows = "other").

### 19. PII Self-Doxxing Outreach Lane  ·  partial  ·  NEW
Surface posts where CUSTOMERS exposed their own Aadhaar/PAN/card, masked preview + "reach out,
get it deleted, secure the account". **49 posts pii_present, structured pii_types (aadhaar 11, account 10).**
Proactive customer protection + DPDP — no planned rail covers it. Never render raw text.

## Cross-cutting data facts worth acting on regardless of UI

1. **963 vader-scored rows all have `emotion='joy'`** — a degenerate default. Either re-classify them through the LLM or hide emotion on lexicon rows. This is a data-quality bug, not just a display choice.
2. **recommended_team='none' on 61.6%** — auto-routing covers 38%, not "most". Any "routed to owning team" copy overstates.
3. **Engagement is Twitter-only** — every velocity/reach/ratio feature silently covers ~49% of the corpus. Label them "Top tweets", not "Top posts".
4. **The planned emerging-cluster banner returns zero rows** — replace it with the Trend Anomaly Rail (#12).
